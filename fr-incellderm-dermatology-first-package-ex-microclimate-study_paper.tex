\begin{abstract}
Background: The French skincare market demands rigorous validation of active ingredient efficacy under varying microclimatic conditions, particularly regarding skin barrier integrity. This study evaluates the INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX (Booster \& Serum Set), focusing on its patented '17 Amino Acid Complex', 'Bacillus Ferment', and 'Giant BYoungPool' delivery technology. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 consumers and B2B professionals across Paris, Lyon, and Marseille assessed texture, absorption, scent neutrality, and perceived efficacy using Likert scales and Net Promoter Score (NPS) metrics. Quantitative Results: The composite mean score was 3.77/5.0 with a Top-2-Box satisfaction rate of 69.8\%. Scent neutrality achieved the highest approval (Mean 4.12, Top-2-Box 99\%), while confidence in EU regulatory compliance reached 94.5\% (Mean 4.2). However, the NPS remained negative (-9.0), driven by price sensitivity (Top Barrier: High Price Point 100\%) and skepticism towards advanced delivery claims (Giant BYoungPool Mean 3.23). Strategic Implications: While technical credibility is high among dermatologists and pharmacists, mass-market adoption requires addressing value perception and simplifying ingredient communication for the French consumer base.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, INCELLDERM, Microclimate Adaptation, Skin Barrier Retention, EU CPNP Compliance, French Consumer Behavior
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
The INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX utilizes a proprietary blend of 17 Amino Acids and Bacillus Ferment to reinforce the stratum corneum. The formulation incorporates Madecassoside and Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4 for anti-inflammatory and anti-aging synergies, delivered via Giant BYoungPool technology to enhance penetration of Acetyl Hexapeptide-8. The study methodology involved a cross-sectional survey of 200 participants in France, segmented by city microclimate (Paris: Cold-Dry; Lyon: Continental; Marseille: Mediterranean Humid). Participants included C-end consumers and B2B aesthetic clinic owners. Data collection focused on sensory attributes (texture, scent), functional performance (hydration duration, absorption), and trust factors (patent credibility, regulatory compliance).
\section{2. Quantitative Metrics \& Microclimate Adaptation}
Overall satisfaction yielded a composite mean of 3.77, with 69.8\% of respondents selecting 'Agree' or 'Strongly Agree'. Texture appropriateness for high-humidity zones scored 3.51 (Top-2-Box 59\%), indicating room for improvement in lightweight feel during summer months. Absorption speed was rated higher at 3.78 (Top-2-Box 74\%). Notably, 60\% of users reported hydration lasting 8-12 hours, supporting the '12-Hour Barrier Retention' claim. Scent profile received exceptional marks (Mean 4.12, Top-2-Box 99\%), aligning with French preferences for neutral, non-intrusive fragrances. However, belief in the efficacy of Giant BYoungPool technology lagged significantly (Mean 3.23, Top-2-Box 23.5\%), suggesting a knowledge gap regarding advanced delivery systems.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Segment analysis reveals distinct preference patterns. Affluent/High Net Worth individuals showed the highest satisfaction (Mean 3.96), compared to Lower-Middle Income groups (Mean 3.56). B2B stakeholders, specifically Aesthetic Clinic Owners, demonstrated strong endorsement (Mean 3.98), valuing patent credibility (100\% selection rate as purchase driver). In contrast, C-end consumers were more sensitive to price barriers (100\% cited 'High Price Point' as an adoption barrier). MBTI data indicates that analytical types (INTJ, ISTJ) valued mechanism clarity but remained skeptical of unverified claims, while feeling types (ISFJ, INFP) prioritized sensory comfort and ethical alignment. Post-procedure fragile skin types reported slightly lower scores (Mean 3.81) due to heightened sensitivity concerns.
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Strategy}
Trust in EU regulatory compliance was exceptionally high (Mean 4.2, Top-2-Box 94.5\%), reflecting confidence in the product's safety labeling and ingredient transparency. Despite this, the Net Promoter Score (NPS) stood at -9.0, primarily driven by detractors citing cost-benefit imbalance. To improve regional strategy, RIMAN must leverage the high trust in 'Patent Credibility' (100\% influence) while mitigating 'Ingredient Complexity' (96.5\% barrier) through simplified educational content. Pricing strategies should consider tiered entry points for the Middle-Income segment, which constitutes the largest demographic block (n=78). Furthermore, localized reviews are critical, as 'Lack of Local Reviews' was cited by 65.5\% of potential adopters as a hesitation factor.

\section{Conclusion}
The INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX demonstrates robust clinical potential in the French market, particularly regarding scent neutrality and regulatory trust. However, the negative NPS highlights a critical disconnect between premium pricing and perceived value among mass-market consumers. Success in France requires bridging the gap between scientific complexity (Giant BYoungPool, Peptides) and tangible user benefits (hydration duration, texture). By targeting B2B channels where patent credibility is highly valued and simplifying ingredient narratives for C-end users, RIMAN can convert high technical appreciation into broader market adoption.
